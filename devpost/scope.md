# UNSTUCK

A minimal AI-powered web application that turns overwhelming thoughts into one small, concrete next action and a short focus session.

## The Unique Kernel
Reduce the decision of where to start to one immediately actionable step, then help the user begin it with a 5–10 minute timer. The central proof is the transition from overwhelm to action.

## Who It's For
Students and people struggling with task initiation or overwhelm, who have something to do but need help identifying a manageable starting point. Their current tools or coping strategies have not been established and are not needed to define this experiment.

## The Core Loop
Open UNSTUCK → describe what is overwhelming you → receive one small, concrete action from a real AI API → start a 5–10 minute focus timer → mark the action complete or retry. Repeat when another starting point is needed. The precise retry behavior will be defined in the PRD.

## Inspiration & Identity
Simple, responsive interface. No visual references or further aesthetic preferences supplied; the PRD will settle essential presentation choices.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Demonstrate a small working thought-to-action-to-focus loop and prepare the required hackathon submission without expanding the proof-of-concept boundary.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Proposed finish line for review: a person opens the app on a phone or desktop, describes an overwhelming task, and sees one concrete starting action generated by a real AI request. They can start a short timer and mark the action complete or retry. A short demo can show the meaningful transition from a vague concern to a specific action and the timer starting, without waiting through a full focus session.

Basic error handling lets the user recover from empty input or a failed AI request without losing their entered text. The timer and completion flow can be checked separately through a full session during verification.

Submission preparation includes the required short demo video and public GitHub repository. Deployment is optional. Keep the project sized for the curriculum's target of 2–4 hours of active work; this is a planning target, not a delivery guarantee.

## The POC Boundary
- Simple responsive interface for the core loop.
- Real AI API integration that recommends one small, concrete next action.
- Short focus timer within the requested 5–10 minute range.
- Mark the action complete or retry.
- Basic validation, loading feedback, and recoverable errors.
- No persistence requirement; the proof of concept works within the current session.

Detailed screens, timer controls, retry semantics, and edge cases belong in the PRD. Stack and AI provider choices belong in the technical spec.

## Later
No later features committed. Reconsider additions only after the working proof of concept and required submission are complete.

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts: unnecessary to demonstrate the core loop.
- Databases: no saved records are needed for this proof of concept.
- Notifications: the focus session can remain on screen.
- Task history: tracking past work adds persistence and interface scope.
- Complex dashboards: they do not help prove the transition to one action.
- Other optional features: protect the smallest functioning proof of concept and submission timeline.
